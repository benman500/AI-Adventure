# Backgrounds

## Purpose

Defines character backgrounds as **upbringing before entering cultivation**. Backgrounds grant starting advantages; they are not reincarnation stories, not rigid classes, and must never permanently lock professions.

## Confirmed design

- The player does **not** reincarnate.
- Background represents **upbringing before entering cultivation**.
- Background is **not a class**.
- Background creates **opportunities, not destiny** (see [GAME_PRINCIPLES.md](GAME_PRINCIPLES.md)).
- Initial backgrounds:
  - Merchant's Child
  - Alchemist's Apprentice
  - Blacksmith's Apprentice
  - Hunter
  - Scholar
  - Farmer
  - Doctor
  - Street Urchin
  - Beast Keeper
  - Minor Noble
- Background provides:
  - **Knowledge**
  - **Starting skills**
  - **Contacts**
  - **Opportunities**
  - **Reputation**
  - **Starting possessions**
- The player may later learn **every profession**.
- Background changes **how** the player reaches the Boundless Foundation story event and provides **unique intros**—**not whether** the event exists. Every background reaches the Boundless choice ([BOUNDLESS_FOUNDATION.md](BOUNDLESS_FOUNDATION.md)).
- Cultivation and profession remain separate.

## Proposed details

Concrete item names are **sparse and provisional**.

### Shared benefit categories

| Category | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Knowledge | What the character already understands |
| Starting skills | Early trained capabilities (not exclusive professions) |
| Contacts | People or networks who respond to them |
| Opportunities | Early jobs, introductions, locations |
| Reputation | How relevant circles initially regard them |
| Starting possessions | Tools, goods, currency, materials |

### Merchant's Child

Raised around ledgers, negotiation, and the flow of goods.

- Knowledge: trade custom, basic appraisal
- Starting skills: haggling, basic bookkeeping
- Contacts: local traders, caravan acquaintances
- Opportunities: consignments, market introductions
- Reputation: known among nearby merchants as “one of theirs”
- Possessions: modest seed money or trade goods (provisional)

### Alchemist's Apprentice

Trained near furnaces and shelves of unstable mixtures.

- Knowledge: basic materia medica / safety habits
- Starting skills: apprentice-level preparation caution
- Contacts: workshop master, herb sellers
- Opportunities: herb runs, workshop errands
- Reputation: junior trust within a pill/herb circle
- Possessions: apprentice tools or leftover ingredients (provisional)

### Blacksmith's Apprentice

Grew up with hammer rhythm and durable workmanship.

- Knowledge: metals, temper, tool care
- Starting skills: basic forge assistance / repair
- Contacts: forge master, material suppliers
- Opportunities: repair work, commissions
- Reputation: known as useful hands at a forge
- Possessions: common tools or a serviceable blade (provisional)

### Hunter

Comfortable with terrain, tracks, and dangerous fauna.

- Knowledge: wilderness travel, tracking
- Starting skills: stalking, field dressing
- Contacts: wardens, village hunters
- Opportunities: ranging contracts, material gathers
- Reputation: reliable in wilder villages
- Possessions: hunting gear; provisions (provisional)

### Scholar

Trained in texts, records, and argument.

- Knowledge: literacy depth, local history / forms
- Starting skills: research, drafting
- Contacts: tutors, clerks, archivists
- Opportunities: copying work, examinations, research favors
- Reputation: literate dependable helper among clerks
- Possessions: writings or study materials (provisional)

### Farmer

Knows seasons, soil, scarcity, and patient labor.

- Knowledge: crops, weather signs, rural custom
- Starting skills: field labor, basic husbandry
- Contacts: village networks, itinerant traders
- Opportunities: land labor, surplus trade
- Reputation: solid worker among rural peers
- Possessions: humble tools; food stores (provisional)

### Doctor

Trained to diagnose and stabilize without careless harm.

- Knowledge: diagnosis, wound care, treatment materia
- Starting skills: basic treatment
- Contacts: clinic ties, patient networks
- Opportunities: paid treatment, crisis aid
- Reputation: trusted for practical care locally
- Possessions: medical kit (provisional)

### Street Urchin

Survived by noticing exits, risks, and powerful people’s blind spots.

- Knowledge: urban underbelly, rumor routes
- Starting skills: stealth, streetwise awareness
- Contacts: informal networks, fences
- Opportunities: tip-offs, irregular jobs
- Reputation: known face in alleys; mixed trust
- Possessions: little wealth (provisional)

### Beast Keeper

Raised among pens, scent, and temperaments.

- Knowledge: husbandry, beast temper
- Starting skills: handling, basic training cues
- Contacts: stables, caravan handlers
- Opportunities: caretaking, living cargo escort
- Reputation: reliable with beasts among handlers
- Possessions: tack/feed or minor companion animal (provisional; companion rules unresolved)

### Minor Noble

Born near privilege without owning its summit.

- Knowledge: etiquette, status games, clan/estate custom
- Starting skills: courtly speech, reading rooms
- Contacts: retainers, peer families, clerks
- Opportunities: banquets, sponsorships, political errands
- Reputation: minor name recognition in local gentry
- Possessions: finer clothes; introduction letters; modest stipend (provisional)

### Hard rule (restated)

Background benefits **must not** prevent later study of any profession the game supports.

## Out of scope / non-goals

- Reincarnation, transmigration, or “previous life memories” as the default player origin.
- Full itemized inventories for every background.
- Unique locked destiny plots per background.
- Ranking backgrounds into optimal combat tiers by birth.

## Unresolved design questions

- Mechanical weight of skills vs reputation vs knowledge tags?
- MVP: full unique packages vs shared templates with flavor?
- Differentiation depth between Doctor and Alchemist's Apprentice?
- How much privilege Minor Noble keeps without breaking early scarcity?
- Singular background only, or hybrids later?

## Expansion notes

- Add backgrounds as data packages using the same categories.
- Profession eligibility stays global.
